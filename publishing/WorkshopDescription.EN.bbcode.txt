[h1]SuQing's Freshness Fridge[/h1]
Build it from the native construction menu and connect mains power, a generator or a supported Live Wire grid. Place food directly inside and choose Seal Fridge and Start Restoration. The contents stay locked until the fridge reaches the configured powered time, then the food is restored together and the fridge unlocks.

[h2]Construction and settings[/h2]
[list]
[*]Uses four small metal sheets, two electric wires, one alarm clock, one digital watch, one pocket watch and one classic wristwatch. Bring a screwdriver, welding mask and one torch use.
[*]Sandbox capacity defaults to 200 and ranges from 50 to 1000. The fridge's separate batch duration defaults to 12 game hours, with a range of 0.25–24.
[*]Progress accrues while the fridge is loaded and power is confirmed. Losing power, leaving the area and closing the game pause progress. The fridge tracks the timer during the wait and processes food together at the start and finish.
[*]Load standard perishable food directly. Remove burnt food, tainted food, fertilized eggs and unsupported items. A separate sandbox setting controls rotten food. Finish active batches before disabling the mod or rolling back.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Version 0.2.0-rc.3 passed offline B42.22.2 single-player validation. Optional integrations include Live Wire 1.7.2, Viewpoint, the Freshness Lunchbox and Loot Tracker 1.8.5. In-game inventory, power changes, save and reload, and frame rate remain for acceptance testing.

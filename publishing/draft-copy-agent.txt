===== 中文 Steam BBCode 介绍 =====
[h1]蘇清的返鲜冰箱[/h1]
把仍然存在的可腐坏食物放入独立返鲜冰箱，在通电与开启返鲜时，逐步恢复它们的新鲜度。采用原生建造、容器与家具搬运流程，附带四向精灵以及可选的独立 Viewpoint 模型。

[h2]如何建造与使用[/h2]
[list]
[*]打开原生建造菜单，选择「蘇清的返鲜冰箱」；没有额外技能门槛。
[*]消耗：小金属板×4、电线×2，以及闹钟、电子表、怀表、经典指针腕表各1件。
[*]工具：螺丝刀、焊接面罩保留；焊枪消耗1原生用量并保留工具本体。电子表接受6种原版版本，经典腕表接受8种原版版本。
[*]接入市电或发电机供电。建成后默认开启返鲜；右键「关闭返鲜／开启返鲜」切换。关闭返鲜后仍遵循普通冰箱的冷藏规则。
[*]食物必须直接放入冰箱；装在冰箱内其他包中的食物不会处理。多件符合条件的食物各自按其原生新鲜期计算。
[/list]

[h2]沙盒设置[/h2]
[list]
[*]启用返鲜：默认开启。
[*]基础容量：默认200，整数范围50–1000；适用于新建与已加载的本模组冰箱。调小容量不会删除内容物，超容时按原版规则限制继续放入。
[*]恢复一个完整新鲜期所需时间：默认10游戏小时，范围0.25–1000；数值越小越快。每个有效游戏小时净减少「该食物的原生新鲜期÷此设置」的食物年龄，最低为0。它不表示所有腐坏程度都固定10小时恢复。
[*]允许恢复腐坏食物：默认开启，仅作用于仍然存在且符合条件的食物。
[/list]

[h2]返鲜范围与时间[/h2]
只结算当前单人会话中连续观察到、已加载且通电的时间。退出游戏不计时；离开加载范围、停用模组及未知供电区间不补返鲜。单次超过15游戏分钟的异常时钟跳变会重新计时，不补算该区间。

返鲜只改变食物年龄，不净化毒素或污染，不修复烧焦，也不改变生熟、营养、配方内容及吃剩份量。受精蛋、无标准保鲜期的食物不处理。已被删除或替换的物品不会重建。

[h2]腐坏食物与兼容限制[/h2]
原版腐坏食物自动删除关闭时，当前候选不再通过临时降低年龄保护食品，只暂时暂停其已有的腐坏替换规则，并在处理结束、转移或保存时恢复。原版自动删除启用或取值未知时，仍走原有年龄保护路径；该分支在 PZO 跨帧调度下的显示行为尚未完成游戏内验收。其他模组的删除、替换或直接修改可能影响可恢复范围。

容量桥接仅针对本模组冰箱的正常 Lua 收纳与转移流程。整理有方／杂乱无章按原版规则影响有效容量；直接从 Java 查询或限制容器容量的自动存取模组可能仍受原生100上限影响，需要单独适配。

[h2]搬运、停用与支持范围[/h2]
搬运前清空冰箱，再使用原生家具搬运功能；搬运期间不返鲜，各台开关状态随搬运保留。移除本模组前，请清空并拆除自定义冰箱，正常保存退出。

适用：Project Zomboid 42.21，单人候选版。提供简体中文与英文，保留现有 CH 资源；无需其他模组即可使用核心功能。Viewpoint 仅为可选模型支持，未安装时使用普通四向精灵。

===== English Steam BBCode description =====
[h1]SuQing's Freshness Fridge[/h1]
An independently buildable, movable fridge that gradually restores the freshness of directly stored perishable food while powered and enabled. It uses native construction, containers and furniture moving, with original four-facing sprites and an optional independent Viewpoint model.

[h2]Build and use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Open the native build menu and select “SuQing's Freshness Fridge.” No additional skill requirement is imposed.
[*]Consumed materials: 4 small metal sheets, 2 electric wires, and one each of an alarm clock, digital watch, pocket watch and classic analog wristwatch.
[*]Tools: screwdriver and welding mask are retained. The blowtorch spends one native fuel use and remains intact. Six vanilla digital-watch variants and eight classic analog-watch variants are accepted.
[*]Supply grid or generator power. Restoration starts enabled; right-click “Disable freshness restoration” / “Enable freshness restoration” to toggle it. With restoration disabled, ordinary refrigeration rules apply.
[*]Place food directly inside. Food inside a bag stored in the fridge is excluded. Eligible foods restore individually according to their own native fresh-life intervals.
[/list]

[h2]Sandbox settings[/h2]
[list]
[*]Enable freshness restoration: on by default.
[*]Base capacity: 200 by default, integer range 50–1000. Applies to new and loaded appliances. Reducing capacity never deletes contents; normal over-capacity insertion limits apply.
[*]Game hours to restore one full fresh-life interval: 10 by default, range 0.25–1000. Lower is faster. Each credited game hour subtracts “that food's native fresh-life interval ÷ this setting” from its age, to a minimum of zero. This is not a promise that every rotten item becomes fresh in exactly 10 hours.
[*]Allow restoration of rotten food: on by default; only existing eligible items can recover.
[/list]

[h2]Restoration and time accounting[/h2]
Credit requires continuously observed, loaded and powered intervals in the current single-player session. Time spent outside the game earns no credit. Unloaded, mod-absent and unverified-power intervals receive no catch-up. A single clock jump exceeding 15 game minutes is re-anchored without restoration credit.

Restoration changes food age. It does not remove poison or contamination, repair burning, or change cooking state, nutrition, ingredients or remaining portions. Fertilized eggs and foods without standard shelf-life thresholds are excluded. Deleted or replaced items are not reconstructed.

[h2]Rotten-food and compatibility limits[/h2]
When native automatic rotten-food removal is disabled, this candidate no longer temporarily reduces age for protection. Only an existing per-item rotten replacement rule is temporarily suspended, then restored after processing, transfer or saving. Enabled or unknown native removal policies retain the previous age-guard path; its display behavior under PZO staggered callbacks still requires in-game acceptance. Other mods that delete, replace or directly edit food can affect what remains recoverable.

The scoped capacity bridge covers this fridge's normal Lua storage and transfer paths. Organized/Disorganized use native effective-capacity rules. Automation mods querying or restricting capacity directly through Java may still encounter the native 100 cap and require separate adaptation.

[h2]Moving, removal and support[/h2]
Empty the fridge before native furniture pickup. A carried appliance does not restore food, and its individual restoration switch survives moving. Before removing this mod, empty and dismantle its custom appliances, then save and exit normally.

Target: Project Zomboid 42.21, single-player candidate. Simplified Chinese and English are included, with existing CH resources retained. Core functionality requires no external mod. Viewpoint is optional; ordinary four-facing sprites work without it.

Existing offline regression and asset-readback records are retained. This preparation pass only checked files and text; it did not rerun tests or start the game. Menu display, collision, power transitions, save/reload and Viewpoint visuals remain subject to user-operated in-game acceptance. Multiplayer support, perfect collision and completed Viewpoint gameplay acceptance are not claimed.

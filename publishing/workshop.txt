description=[h1]SuQing's Freshness Fridge[/h1]
description=Bites the Dust—bring back the freshness! An independently buildable, movable fridge that gradually restores directly stored perishable food while powered and enabled. Eligible items restore together.
description=
description=[h2]Build and use[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Select “SuQing's Freshness Fridge” in the native build menu. No extra skill threshold is imposed.
description=[*]Consumes 4 small metal sheets, 2 electric wires, and one each of an alarm clock, digital watch, pocket watch and classic analog wristwatch.
description=[*]Requires a screwdriver, welding mask and blowtorch. The first two are kept; the torch spends one native fuel use and is retained. Listed vanilla watch variants are accepted.
description=[*]Supply grid or generator power and place food directly inside. Restoration starts enabled; right-click to toggle it. Ordinary refrigeration rules apply when restoration is off.
description=[*]Empty the appliance before native furniture pickup. Its individual switch is retained; a carried fridge does not restore food.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Separate sandbox settings[/h2]
description=Restoration is enabled by default. Capacity defaults to 200 (50–1000). Restoring one full fresh-life interval takes 10 game hours by default (0.25–1000). Rotten-food support is on. Lower is faster; different ages do not finish at one fixed time.
description=
description=Credit requires continuously observed loaded, powered and enabled time in the current session. Unloaded, offline, unverified-power intervals and abnormal clock jumps earn no catch-up. Lowering capacity does not delete contents; normal over-capacity insertion limits apply.
description=
description=[h2]Scope and compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Only directly stored Food with standard shelf-life thresholds is processed, not food in nested bags. Burnt food, fertilized eggs and unsupported shelf lives are excluded. Poison, contamination, cooking state, ingredients and remaining portions are unchanged. Deleted/replaced food is not recreated.
description=[*]Core functions need no external mod. Four-facing sprites are included; Viewpoint is optional 3D support whose visuals/collision require gameplay confirmation.
description=[*]Capacity support covers normal storage/transfers for this fridge. Automation querying capacity directly through Java may still encounter the native 100 cap. Organized/Disorganized keep native effective-capacity rules.
description=[*]This candidate includes the relevant display correction when native automatic rotten-food removal is disabled. Enabled/unknown removal policies with PZO still need display validation. World settings are never changed automatically.
description=[*]For 42.21 single-player; multiplayer is not claimed. Empty and dismantle custom fridges before disabling, then save and exit normally.
description=[/list]
